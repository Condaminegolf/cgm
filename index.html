Preferred Lies

Preferred lies are in effect throughout the course. A ball in a poor lie may be lifted and placed within one scorecard length of its original position, no nearer the hole. It must remain in the same general area of the course and must not be placed on a putting surface. Where practical, place the ball on the nearest available tuft of grass rather than bare ground.

Power Lines

If a player's ball strikes an overhead power line crossing the course, the stroke is cancelled. Replay the stroke from its original position without penalty.

Sand Greens

When on a sand green, mark and lift your ball, smooth a putting path from your ball to the hole, and replace the ball on the smoothed path at its original position or an equivalent position. Complete your putt. After holing out, remove sand from the hole and re-rake the putting surface for the next group.

Out of Bounds

Out of bounds includes the Campdraft Arena, all fenced areas and external fences. The club document states that a ball out of bounds incurs a one-stroke penalty and must be replayed from the previous position (stroke and distance), or as permitted under an adopted committee local rule.

Course Etiquette

Keep the course enjoyable for everyone. Leave sand greens in good condition, rake bunkers, and respect fellow players, volunteers and course equipment.

Campdraft Arena — No Shots Over the Arena

Do not play shots over the Campdraft Arena. For the safety of people, livestock and infrastructure, players must not attempt to hit over this area under any circumstances. Damage to campdraft arena equipment may be invoiced for the cost of repairs.

Source: Condamine Sports Club, Hols.pdf, page 1. For competition rulings consult the club committee.

GPS, accuracy and fairway notes

Location accuracy is the phone's reported horizontal accuracy in metres (approximately a confidence radius), not guaranteed rangefinder precision. Auto-hole detection checks your position against mapped fairways and uses nearest-tees/greens only within a limited range. Close or overlapping holes can be ambiguous; you can override the hole manually. Club course markers and fairway boundaries are read-only. Automatic imagery uses Esri through zoom 18 and Queensland aerial at zoom 19 and above. Internet access is needed for live imagery; the supplied aerial image remains available offline.
