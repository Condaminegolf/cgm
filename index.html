GPS, accuracy and fairway notes

Location accuracy is the phone's reported horizontal accuracy in metres (approximately a confidence radius), not guaranteed rangefinder precision. Auto-hole detection checks your position against mapped fairways and uses nearest-tees/greens only within a limited range. Close or overlapping holes can be ambiguous; you can override the hole manually. Club course markers and fairway boundaries are read-only. Internet access is needed for satellite maps and the Leaflet library.
